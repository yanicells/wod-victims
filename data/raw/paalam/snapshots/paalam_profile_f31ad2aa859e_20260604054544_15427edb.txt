THE Bacolod City Police Station 2 is conducting a thorough investigation in the killing of a 48-year-old drug surrenderer in Purok Sigay, Barangay 2, in Bacolod City Sunday evening, August 30, 2020.
Police identified the fatality as Norman Sola of Barangay 2.
Police Captain Leo Estopa, chief of Police Station 2, said they are still gathering pieces of evidence to determine the identity of the suspect in the killing of Sola.
The police are also coordinating with the family of the victim.

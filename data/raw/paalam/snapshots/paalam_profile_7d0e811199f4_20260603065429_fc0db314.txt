Cavite PPO intensified campaign against illegal drugs likewise resulted in neutralization of one person identified as one EDWIN CALLOS and arrested 19 others out of 13 drug operation and 2 other drug related operation conducted. During the operation , police authorities recovered 66 pieces small HSTPS, 1 piece medium HSTPS and other drug paraphernalias.
Sex: Male
Incident Details
Killed in police operation Date of Incident: May 20, 2021 Time of Incident: 12:30 am Location of Incident: General Mariano Alvarez, Cavite
Source(s)
https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/1434356/2-drug-suspects-slain-in-cavite-buy-busts https://www.facebook.com/cavitepoliceprovincialoffice/posts/793889401526604
Edwin Callos died in a sting operation that ended in a firefight in Barangay Granados in General Mariano Alvarez town around 12:30 a.m. Thursday.
The police reportedly found from the slain suspect one caliber .38 revolver loaded with three bullets.
Callos allegedly yielded two sachets of shabu (crystal meth) weighing six grams and worth about P40,000 in Dangerous Drugs Board value.

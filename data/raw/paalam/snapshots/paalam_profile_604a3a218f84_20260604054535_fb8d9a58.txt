A teenager was gunned down by motorcycle-riding assailants in Caloocan City last week.
Joshua Evangelista, 19, spent the last three years of his life in a juvenile jail until he was killed along C-3 Road in Barangay 28.
In a Facebook post, Fr. Flavie Villanueva said Evangelista was described by his family as “helpful, kind” and a “homebuddy” since he was freed from detention in March last year.
Villanueva said his death continued the pattern of shady killings of drug suspects who had been freed from jail.

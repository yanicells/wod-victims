Suspected drug pushers were killed in a buy-bust operation in Camarines Sur.
Master Sergeant Tobias Bongon, Naga City police spokesperson, said Victor Lawanan and Andrew Flores were killed in Barangay Tinago around 1 a.m. Wednesday.
Bongon said after selling shabu (crystal meth) worth P5,000, the suspects noticed the presence of the police officers and drew their guns.
Lawanan and Flores fired at the anti-narcotics operatives, prompting them to retaliate and killing the suspects instantly.
The police seized a caliber. 45 and .38 guns from the suspects, Bongon added.

A drug suspect was killed in a buy-bust operations in Cavite province.
Colonel Marlon Santos, Cavite police chief, reported at 11 a.m. Tuesday that Larry Miranda was killed in a sting that ended in an encounter in Barangay Bucana Sasahan in Naic town.
The police did not provide details on the operation that led to the death of the alleged drug suspect.

Aldrin Singcosa

  • admin
  • 08 Jun 2021
  • 0 Comments
Noong Miyerkules, iniulat din ni Col. Marlon Santos, hepe ng pulisya ng Cavite, ang pagkamatay ng isa pang suspek na si Aldrin Singcosa alyas “Bumbay.” Napatay siya sa isang engkwentro sa pagitan ng kapulisan sa Barangay Punta, Tanza. Narekober umano ng mga awtoridad mula sa napatay na suspek ang isang Colt MK4 caliber .45 pistol na may serial number, isang Colt MK4 caliber .45 pistol na walang serial number, dalawang magazine na may 10 bala, at apat na fired fired case.
  • Alias: “Bumbay”
  • Sex: Male
Incident Details
  • Killed in police operation
  • Date of Incident: May 19, 2021
  • Location of Incident: Tanza , Cavite
Source(s)
  • https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/1434356/2-drug-suspects-slain-in-cavite-buy-busts
  • https://www.philstar.com/pilipino-star-ngayon/probinsiya/2021/05/20/2099486/big-time-tulak-todas-sa-buy-bust

Police reported the death of drug suspect Aldrin Singcosa alias “Bumbay.”

Col. Marlon Santos, Cavite police chief, said Singcosa was killed after he engaged the policemen in a firefight in Barangay Punta 1 in Tanza town.

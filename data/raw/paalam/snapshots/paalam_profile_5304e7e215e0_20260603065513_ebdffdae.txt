Incident Details
Killed in police operation in public Date of Incident: May 22, 2021 Location of Incident: San Ildefonso, Bulacan
Source(s)
https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/1435611/3-drug-suspects-killed-in-separate-buy-busts-in-bulacan
Three suspected drug traders were killed after they reportedly engaged policemen in a shootout in San Ildefonso town at dawn on Saturday (May 22).
Col. Lawrence Cajipe, Bulacan police director, identified one of the slain suspects as alias “Dagul” who resisted arrest after he was caught selling alleged “shabu” (crystal meth) to an undercover agent in Barangay Pinaod around 1:20 a.m.
The suspect and his unidentified companion fired shots at the policemen who shot back. Both suspects died instantly.

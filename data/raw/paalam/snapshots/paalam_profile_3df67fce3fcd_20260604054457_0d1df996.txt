Another drug suspect, who was identified as Jeremie Garcia, was killed in Barangay Sapang Palay in the City of San Jose del Monte around 1 a.m. on Sunday after he engaged policemen in a shootout.
The unidentified companions of the suspects managed to escape, police said.
Sex: Male
Incident Details
Killed in police operation in public Date of Incident: November 6, 2020 Location of Incident: San Jose del Monte, Bulacan

Sa siyudad naman ng San Jose del Monte, patay rin ang suspek na si Jeremie Garcia alyas Diego, kung saan nakuha naman sa kaniya ang isang pakete ng hinihinalang shabu, isang revolver at motorsiklo.
Arestado rin sa pagkakasangkot sa droga ang 13 iba pa na mula sa mga bayan ng Calumpit, Guiguinto, Malolos, Meycauayan, San Miguel, San Rafael at lungsod ng San Jose Del Monte.

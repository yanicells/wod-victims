Incident Details
Killed in police operation in public Date of Incident: October 25, 2020 Time of Incident: 5:30 am Location of Incident: Navotas, Metro Manila
Source(s)

Oct. 25, 2020, about 5:30 am, Tangos, Navotas. Jocel Sales, 29 yo. was playing Kara y Cruz with friends along B. Cruz St. in North Tangos, Navotas when a motorcycle stopped and a man wearing a helmet, black jacket and cargo shorts got off, sensing danger Jocel got up and left but the assailant caught up, Sales begged for his life, but to no avail and was shot and killed on the spot. The family said Sales was a substance abuser and was just recently released from detention.
